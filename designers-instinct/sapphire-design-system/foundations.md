# Foundations

## Experience principle
Every surface must communicate control before decoration. The Director should feel that high-value commercial conditions are visible, traceable and actionable.

The approved Actions execution canvas is the current cross-workspace baseline for density, shell geometry, working-surface hierarchy and interaction restraint.

## Visual hierarchy
Maximum three emphasis levels per screen:
1. Primary commercial condition or decision.
2. Supporting context and movement.
3. Background detail and metadata.

## Spacing
Use an 8px base grid. Core scale: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 40, 48, 64, 80.

## Radius
- compact controls: 8px
- standard controls: 12px
- cards and panels: 16px
- major command surfaces: 20px
- overlays: 20px

Avoid excessive pill shapes. Pills are reserved for statuses, filters and compact metadata.

## Elevation
Elevation must be restrained and functional.
- Level 0: base canvas
- Level 1: standard card
- Level 2: active or floating panel
- Level 3: modal, command palette or critical overlay

Use border contrast before shadow. No broad neon glow around ordinary cards.

## Density
Sapphire targets Bloomberg-level usefulness with Apple-level restraint. Dense information is acceptable when hierarchy remains obvious. Whitespace must create decision clarity, not emptiness.

## Materials
- matte titanium surfaces
- low-reflective graphite panels
- fine steel-blue separators
- limited sapphire illumination
- minimal transparency
- subtle polished-chrome highlights on opposing card corners
- restrained low-contrast gradients behind headers and focused working regions

Chrome and gradients communicate premium material and focus only. They may not carry commercial status or compete with critical information.

## Accessibility
- WCAG 2.2 AA minimum
- visible keyboard focus
- minimum 44px touch target for mobile/Capacitor
- colour is never the sole carrier of meaning
- reduced-motion mode must preserve comprehension
- critical information must remain legible at 200% zoom
