# Commercial Semantics

## Decision contract
Every major card, alert and intelligence surface must answer:
- What is happening?
- Why does it matter?
- What is the next valid action?

## Semantic classes
- Information: verified context without required intervention.
- Progress: confirmed movement toward a commercial outcome.
- Opportunity: evidence-backed upside requiring evaluation.
- Attention: a condition that may need action.
- Warning: material uncertainty, delay or exposure.
- Critical: blocked, unsafe, unauthorised or materially deteriorating.
- AI inference: machine-generated interpretation with confidence and evidence links.

## Financial presentation
Every monetary value must expose currency, period, definition and status where relevant. Potential value, probability-weighted value, expected commission, realised revenue and value-at-risk must never be visually conflated.

## Intelligence presentation
AI and Market Radar outputs must show:
- recommendation or signal type;
- confidence;
- evidence recency;
- provenance;
- uncertainty or contradiction;
- action route;
- human authority boundary.

## Authority
Visibility does not imply permission. Workflow state does not imply approval. Buttons and menus must reflect actual server-authorised capability.

## Data visualisation
Charts must answer a commercial question. Every metric must drill into its definition and owning workspace. Decorative charts, unexplained scores and colour-only conclusions are prohibited.
