# Dashboard Landing Principles

## Doctrine

The Sapphire dashboard is the commercial command centre.

It is not:

- a CRM homepage;
- a reporting page;
- every module squeezed into one screen;
- a decorative analytics surface;
- a substitute for the workspaces that own operational detail.

The dashboard exists to answer four questions immediately:

1. What is happening?
2. What needs attention?
3. What is progressing?
4. What commercial value is emerging?

## Role of the dashboard

The dashboard compresses the state of the commercial system into a decision-ready view. It reveals movement, risk, opportunity, blockage, and value without attempting to reproduce the full content of any workspace.

Every widget summarises.

Every summary drills into its owning workspace.

The dashboard may reference records from across Sapphire, but it does not become the primary owner of those records.

## Widget requirements

Every dashboard widget must:

- answer a clear commercial question;
- identify the owning workspace;
- show only the minimum information required for orientation or action;
- distinguish normal progress from exceptions requiring attention;
- provide a meaningful drill-down destination;
- lead to a record, queue, workflow, decision, or workspace;
- avoid duplicating the full operational interface of another surface.

A widget must be removed or redesigned when it cannot explain:

- what changed;
- why it matters;
- what should happen next;
- where the user goes to act.

## Priority hierarchy

The dashboard prioritises:

1. Commercial risks and blocked execution.
2. Time-sensitive actions and decisions.
3. Material progression across opportunities and deals.
4. Emerging commercial value.
5. Contextual performance signals.

Decorative insight never outranks an actionable commercial condition.

## Summary before detail

The landing view should communicate state before exposing complexity. Detail belongs in the workspace responsible for the underlying commercial process.

The dashboard therefore favours:

- exception summaries over exhaustive lists;
- directional signals over raw data dumps;
- active commercial movement over historical decoration;
- next actions over passive observation.

## Authority boundary

The dashboard may recommend, prioritise, and explain. It may not grant authority, bypass permissions, or convert workflow state into approval.

A visible action is not automatically an authorised action.
