# Inbox and Communications Doctrine

## Status

Permanent doctrine for communication inside Sapphire Core OS.

## Governing principle

Sapphire must be capable of becoming the user's primary commercial operating environment.

**Inbox is a first-class Commercial Workspace.**

It is not limited to email and it is not an unstructured message feed.

## Dominant commercial question

Who communicated what, what does it affect, what requires a response, and what commercial record must retain the context?

## Communication scope

Inbox may unify:

- internal Sapphire messages;
- email;
- supplier and buyer correspondence;
- WhatsApp or approved messaging integrations;
- SMS;
- call logs and recordings where lawful;
- voice notes;
- meeting notes;
- finance and banking correspondence;
- document requests and responses;
- system-generated notices.

External channels remain subject to provider capability, consent, retention law and integration policy.

## Thread doctrine

Every communication thread must be capable of linking to one or more:

- people and companies;
- missions and tasks;
- demand and supply records;
- opportunities, matches and deals;
- profiles and acquisition candidates;
- contracts and documents;
- banks, payments and finance records;
- governance or compliance cases.

A message without commercial context may enter Inbox, but Sapphire must support classification and linking before it becomes institutional memory.

## Inbox states

Inbox must distinguish:

- unread;
- requires response;
- awaiting external response;
- assigned;
- converted to action;
- decision required;
- linked but informational;
- resolved;
- archived;
- restricted or privileged.

Read state must not be confused with resolved state.

## Action conversion

Any authorised communication may create:

- a task;
- a mission;
- a follow-up;
- a decision request;
- a document request;
- an opportunity or profile update proposal.

The source message and resulting action must remain traceably linked.

## AI behaviour

AI may:

- summarise threads;
- extract commitments, dates and commercial terms;
- recommend record links;
- draft replies;
- identify unanswered questions;
- detect potential risk or contradiction;
- propose actions.

AI may not:

- send external communications without explicit user authority or approved automation policy;
- silently alter verified commercial terms;
- conceal uncertainty;
- expose restricted communications;
- treat inferred intent as confirmed instruction.

## HOME contract

HOME must include an **Inbox Summary** that reveals:

- messages requiring the Director's response;
- high-value or high-risk conversations;
- overdue external replies;
- new communications affecting active missions, opportunities or deals;
- communication-derived decisions or actions.

The summary drills into Inbox. HOME must not become a full messaging client.

## Ownership

Inbox owns:

- channels and connected identities;
- conversations and threads;
- messages and message versions;
- participants;
- delivery, read and response states;
- attachments as references to Documents;
- communication classifications;
- record links;
- reply obligations;
- communication audit events.

Actions owns resulting work. Documents owns canonical files. Network owns people and companies.

## Implementation contract

The implementation must support:

- canonical conversation and message DDL;
- provider identity and channel mapping;
- deduplication and idempotent ingestion;
- secure attachment references;
- participant- and record-aware RLS;
- RPCs for Director, personal and record-scoped inboxes;
- triggers for response obligations and action proposals;
- realtime updates;
- retention, deletion and legal-hold policy;
- immutable audit of send, edit, link, assignment and resolution events;
- provider-independent internal message identifiers.

Communication context must never exist only in a frontend cache or third-party provider.
