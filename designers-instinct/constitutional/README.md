Constitutional standards bind every human developer, AI agent, design system, prototype and production implementation unless the Director explicitly approves an exception or amendment.

They govern implementation procedure and evidence. They do not grant commercial authority, redefine business doctrine or weaken accessibility, security, permissions or correctness.
