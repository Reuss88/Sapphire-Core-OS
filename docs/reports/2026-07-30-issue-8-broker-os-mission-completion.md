## Broker OS definition

Sapphire OS is a Broker Operating System that uses network, pricing, relationship, and operational intelligence to acquire opportunities, connect buyers and sellers, coordinate deals, and earn subscription and transaction revenue.

## Primary user

The primary user is the broker or operator who coordinates fragmented demand, supply, introducers, closers, fulfilment providers, and pricing evidence.

## Core economic loop

```text
Demand detected
→ suitable supply identified
→ lead qualified
→ opportunity matched
→ closer or automated process converts
→ transaction or agreement occurs
→ fulfilment is coordinated
→ Sapphire records value created
→ commission, fee, margin or subscription is collected
→ network and pricing intelligence improve
```

## Subscription model

- Confirmed direction: tiered operating subscription
- Working price examples, not final public prices: about `£50` for two included capabilities and about `£80` for four included capabilities

## Brokerage revenue model

Candidate structures include percentage commission, fixed introduction fee, success fee, recurring revenue share, margin spread, territory fee, and category-specific fee. Exact rules remain unconfirmed.

## Internal operator model

Reuss uses Sapphire to run broker-led ventures himself and retain the resulting upside. This is core intent, not a side effect.

## Pricing intelligence model

Sapphire is intended to learn from quotes, accepted prices, rejected prices, discounts, commissions, margins, supplier rates, willingness to pay, territory variation, seasonality, and conversion by price point. Live external data coverage remains unverified.

## Network intelligence model

Sapphire learns from governed relationship and transaction history: who knows whom, who introduced whom, who buys from whom, who pays, who fulfils well, who closes effectively, and who has trusted capacity.
